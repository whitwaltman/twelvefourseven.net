Names are technologies in and of themselves. They are made by humans and they do work.

As a trans person of color, I think about names a lot. About what they signal, about who they serve. The name I was given when I was born is extremely gendered. The same goes for my sister. Our names are also extremely Anglocentric (other than our last name).
