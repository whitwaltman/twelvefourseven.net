I have a confusing relationship with memory.

As a child, I was often told that I had a good memory. One anecdote my parents liked to recall was the time I memorized a poem for Chinese school just by phonetics. I had no idea what the words meant. Most of the other kids in my class spoke Mandarin at home with their parents, but mine were always at work and my Dad didn't speak the language.

So maybe I have a good long-term memory. Yet, there are significant chunks of my life I can't remember in anything other than fuzzy detail. It feels like the older I get, the more my window of recollection shrinks. And maybe that's just a natural part of aging. But it kind of feels like there's more to it?
