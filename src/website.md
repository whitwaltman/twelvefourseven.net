I describe this website as my "public notebook". But what does that mean in practice?

Well, I don't really know yet. A piece of writing that's influenced my thinking on the matter is Michael Nielsen's
[How to use a personal website to enhance your ability to think and create?](https://michaelnotebook.com/wn/website_enhance.html).

In the footnotes, he mentions that a metaphor he likes to use to explain the purpose of his site {% aside "which is to help him think well" %} is that of creating an intellectual home for himself: designing and furnishing it, and developing habits that he's comfortable using in it.

Rather than wait until I have a clearer idea of what I want from this website, I've decided to find it through making.
That means that things will change and break sometimes. {% aside "This aspect does frustrate me sometimes, but I've come to accept it as a worthwhile trade-off." %}
