---
title: Why a notebook?
date: '2026-02-21T12:00:00Z'
---

Sometimes I push back against myself and this whole idea of having a "public notebook". Why not just make a blog?

I've never been a particularly skilled writer. My problem isn't having ideas or knowing what to say. I just can't seem to string it all together in a cohesive way.

Let me give you an example. In college, I took a class called "Action Cinema". I don't think I turned a single paper in on time for it (Sorry Dr. Banerjee). I can't exactly remember how long it took me to finish writing my first analytical essay, but it must've been weeks, if not close to 2 months.
