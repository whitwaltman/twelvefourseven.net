I've been interested in pedagogy and epistemology for much of my life, even if I didn't have the language to describe it. A large part of my intrigue stems from my own love for learning, and my frustrations with "traditional" methods of education that, in my opinion, fell short.

I like the idea of instructional scaffolding and am also interested in going deeper on situated cognition. These are just some messy collected notes on stuff related to constructivist theory. Over time, I hope to formalize some of my thoughts through writing and discover new insights and applications of this pedagogical lens.

* * *

I need to revisit Michael Nielsen's [Augmenting Long-term Memory](https://augmentingcognition.com/ltm.html). While it isn't directly related to or about constructivism, it holds a lot of interesting ideas and nods to constructivist ideas in talking about memory and learning.
