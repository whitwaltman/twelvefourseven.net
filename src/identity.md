I often think about digital identity. The topic does interest me, but mostly I think about it from the perspective of self-preservation.

A piece of writing I like on the topic of identity is Wesley Aptekar-Cassel's [IndieWeb Thoughts: POSSE](https://notebook.wesleyac.com/indieweb-thoughts-posse/). They observe that those who have historically built websites and internet infrastructure (straight white men) by default have not had to compartmentalize aspects of their identity or persona into interchangeable fragments. For someone like me, that is very much not the case.

I often wish I could have a single, unified identity. It's quite stressful (though less now as an adult) to maintain informational boundaries in several aspects of your life. But when you're queer and growing up in a religious household, you don't have much of a choice.
